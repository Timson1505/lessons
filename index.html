For Students

YOUR ENGLISH JOURNEY STARTS HERE

Build your English one small step at a time.

Learn useful words, understand grammar, practise reading and use English in everyday situations. Choose a goal, practise regularly and celebrate your progress.

Explore English Lessons

Grow Your Vocabulary

Learn words in context instead of memorising isolated lists. Write your own example sentence for every new word.

Useful word: improveI practise English every day to improve my speaking.

Understand Grammar

Focus on one grammar point at a time. Read the rule, compare examples, then use it in your own sentences.

Present SimpleI study English every evening.She studies English every evening.

Speak with Confidence

Practise out loud, even when you are alone. Describe your day, answer simple questions and repeat useful phrases.

Try saying:“Could you say that again, please?”“In my opinion…”

Read, Listen and Review

Use short texts and audio that match your level. Review yesterday’s words before adding new ones.

Remember:Short, regular practice is more useful than one long study session once in a while.
A SIMPLE DAILY ROUTINE

Your 20-minute English plan

  • 5 minReviewRecall words from previous lessons.
  • 5 minLearnStudy one new grammar rule or 5–10 words.
  • 5 minPractiseComplete an exercise or write 3 sentences.
  • 5 minSpeakRead aloud or talk about your day.
STUDY SMARTER

5 habits that help you improve

  1. Set one small, clear goal for each study session.
  2. Say new words aloud and use them in sentences.
  3. Keep a notebook of mistakes and corrections.
  4. Review vocabulary after 1 day, 3 days and 1 week.
  5. Do not fear mistakes — use them to learn.
FIND YOUR STARTING POINT

Learn at your own level

Start with what you can understand, then gradually increase the challenge.

A1–A2Everyday words, basic grammar and simple conversations.
B1–B2Longer texts, opinions, fluent conversation and more complex grammar.
Keep goingTrack what you learn and return to difficult topics for review.
