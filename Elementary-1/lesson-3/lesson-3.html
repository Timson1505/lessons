Margulan Seisenbai

Margulan Seisenbai is a well-known businessman from Kazakhstan. His story is a strong example that shows how the past and future are connected.

In his childhood, he used to play with toys and spend hours on the playground with friends. Children in those days used to create their own games, because there were no video games, apps or social media. The friendship was real, and every memory stayed in the hearts.

He also used to work on the family farm. Life in the village was not easy. From the farm he learned that success does not come quickly - it must be earned.

In adult age he worked hard and started building his own business. He saw that technology was becoming the key to success. Every device, network and app helped him to grow faster. So he understood the power of change.

Today, he believes the future will bring even beigger changes. Schools will teach online lessons like the "Teacherman" - program instead of books. Artificial Inteligence (AI) will guide business, and every new idea will appear on the screen. People will connect to the internet and Social Medias and every business will depend on apps, internet and smart devices.

His message is: yougn people should not be afraid of the future. Technology will open the doors but hard work must always stay strong. Just like Margulan, the next generation will achieve greater success if they believe in themselves.

Future life

Life has changed a lot over the years. In the past people lived very simply. They used to play with toys outside and spend hours on the playground. People used to meet friends and family face to face to build strong friendships, and they used to share memories by talking not by writing on whatsapp.

Technology was different too. People used to listen to music on big devices. There were not apps, video games or phones with touch screens. Many families used to work on farms in the village and life was slower.

Today the world looks defferent. We use modern devices, play video games and share our lives on Instagram. People can connect through networks, even children study online.

I believe, in the future life will change even more. Schools and universities will use Artificial Inteligence (AI) to teach. People will make friends through apps and robots will prepare foods, drive cars. Our lives will depend more and more on technology. But values like friendship and respect must stay strong.
