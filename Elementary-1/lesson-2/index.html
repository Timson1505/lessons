Exercise 2: Complete the gaps with words from the brackets using "will".

  1. I ________ to help you with your homework. (promise)
  2. They ________ the next week. (schedule)
  3. She ________ what to wear for the party (decide)
  4. We ________ a family trip (arrange)
  5. We ________ for the test tomorrow. (prepare)
  6. They ________ a picnic in the park this weekend. (plan)
  7. Our teacher ________ us to study harder. (expect)
  8. On Friday, we ________ at the mosque. (pray)

My life in 10 years

In 10 years, I will be 28 years old. I plan to finish my studies and start a good job. I hope I will work as a teacher because, I love teaching.

I think that my days will be very busy, so I will make a schedule every week. I will decide carefully how to use my time. I will also arrange meetins with my friends and family.

I promise, I will always respect my parents and help them when they need me. On fridays I will go to the mosque, and I will pray for my family and my future.

I know, the life is not always easy, so I will prepare for the challenges. But I am sure my future will be bright, because I will work hard and stay positive.

The Future Technologies

Hello! My name is Amir, in this letter I want to tell you about the future. I think the world will change a lot because of technology.

In the future cars will fly and people will decide where to go just using their voice command. Robots will prepare food, clean houses and even help children with homework. Lessons will be online and students will decide what to study and when to study, like the "timson's" program. I plan to become an Engineer and I hope I will create new machines to make life easier. I expect that hospitals will use universal cure to treat people.

I promise, I will use technology in a good way. There is a lot of information and I will use the internet to study. If we respect each other and use our knowladge wisely, the future will be bright for everyone.
