    Old and modern life

    In the past, people used to live very differently. They used to repair things themselves, like clothes or furniture, because there were not supermarkets as today. Families used to spend a lot of time together at home, telling stories and celebreating holidays. Children used to attend local school, and after classes, they used to play outside.

    Now, life is very different. Many people get used to using technology everyday. We spend more time on our phones or computers than we used to. We can deliver messages instantly and achieve things faster than our grandparents.

    However, some of our old habits remain. People still celebrate important traditions, but now they mix old customs with new technology. For example, weddings used to be simple ceremonies, but today, we attend traditional and modern parties with music and videos.

    Life has changed. It's interesting to see how people adapt. Old and modern life exist togather and each one is important.

    Kazakh / kyrgyz weddings

    Last year, I attended my brother's wedding, and it was an amazing experience. In the past, kazakh and kyrgyz weddings used to be simple. People used to celebrate at home in the village, everyone participated in preparing food. Gifts used to be handmade, groom's family used to give horses, sheeps, cows or little money as kalym.

    Now, weddings are very different. Many people spend a lot of money on restaurants, parties and music. At my brother's wedding, the event was held in a big and beautiful restaurant and we celebrated late into the night. Guest gave presents, but often it's money instead of horse, sheep and cow. Some older relatives used to find this strange, but others get used to the modern style of wedding. Guests participated in games and dancing.

    It was a beautiful day, and now I see how old habits can get used to new ways, making the celebration unforgettable.
