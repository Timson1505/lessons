Exercise 1: Open the brackets and write words in past simple.

  1. I __________ to the park yesterday. (go)
  2. My brother __________ home late last night. (come)
  3. We __________ pizza for dinner. (have)
  4. She __________ her homework after dinner. (do)
  5. He __________ a present from relatives. (get)
  6. I __________ a beautiful rainbow after the rain. (see)
  7. She __________ my keys yesterday. (take)
  8. You __________ a cake for his birthday. (make)
  9. We __________ a dinner in an expensive restraunt. (eat)
  10. I __________ an interesting book last weekend. (read)

Nurcholpon - the voice of Kyrgyz Opera

Nurcholpon was born in the south of Kyrgyzstan. As a child, she loved music. She played the violin, guitar and sang at school concerts. Her teacher noticed it and said to continue. After school she came to Osh College of Music and later moved to Bishkek to study at the Kyrgyz National Conservatory.

She studied very hard and dreamed about writing music. She also read many books about world classics and opera.

In 2017, She took part in a music contest in Baku. She sang very well and won second place. After that she visited many countries. Nurcholpon met many musicians. Later, she wrote her first opera "Ai Tentek". So, she became the first women to write an opera in Kyrgyz History. Many people saw an opera.

In 2025 she sang a folk song and uploaded it to the internet. Many people wached and like it. She also helped other singers. She gave them advice and explained the music.

My family's weekend.

Last weekend, my family and I had a great time. On Saturday, we stayed at home. My mom cleaned the house and I helped her. My dad fixed the car, and my sister watched a movie. In the evening, we all had dinner together. After dinner, we played games and laughed a lot.

On Sunday, we went to the park. We took some food and sat on the grass. My brother ran and played ball. My parents talked and rested. It was sunny and warm. We enjoyed the day very much.

Our weekend was simple but fun. We spent time together and felt happy.
