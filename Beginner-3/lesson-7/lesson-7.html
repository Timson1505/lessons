Grammar:

- We use "there was/were" to describe environment in numbers in the past!
Singlar Plural
Positive (+) There was a laptop There were 2 laptops
Negative (-) There was not a student There were not 5 students
Question (?) Was there a car? Were there many cars?

I met with my classmates.

Recently, I met with my classmates and talked about school days. We remembered many things from our childhood. There were many funny moments, but also serious ones. I remember, there was an accident near our school. We didn't see it clearly, but some people were there to witness it. The school gave the first aid service, but luckily no one was hurt.

We also talked about strict teacher. He always gave us a lot of homework, but now we understand that he just wanted the best for us. One of my classmates said: once he tried to copy the test and got in trouble.

In my neighbourhood, I had a good neighbour who gave me sweets and told interesting stories. She had a steel gate and I hated the sound it made. My best friend had a pet puppy. We played after school every day.

We also remember how we were afraid of a dark room in the school basemant. Nothing bad ever happend there, but we always imagined something scary.

Those days were full of joy and small adventures. I miss my school sometimes, so I am happy I had those beautiful memories.

My neighbourhood.

I remember my neighbourhood very well. It's small, but it was full of life. There was a little park near my house. It wasn't big, but there were many children playing there every day.

There was a canteen next to the park. The fresh bread was just amazing! There were also two small shops where we could buy candy and juice. I went there with my friends after school.

Across the street, there was a library. It was quiet and peaceful. There were many books, and the librarian was very kind. She always helped me choose good stories to read.

In our neighbourhood, there were many neighbours. There was an old man who gave us apples from his garden. There was a woman who had two cats and one dog. The dog was barking, but very funny!

There was not a supermarket, so we had to walk a bit to buy a big things. But we never mind it. There were so many good mamories in that nieghbourhood. I really loved living there!
