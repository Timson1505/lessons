Grammar:

- Use "want" to talk about things!
- Use "want to" to talk about action!
Want + thing Want to + action
I want a car I want to drive a car
She wants a dress She wants to buy a dress

Jack Ma

Jack Ma, the founder of Alibaba, was not always rich. When he was young, his income was very small. He worked as an English teacher, but his salary was not enough for his expenses. He often had to think carefully about his budget, because he didn’t want to spend too much.

Life was hard. Sometimes he couldn’t pay a bill, and he even thought about taking a loan. But Jack didn’t give up. He said, “I don’t want to live with a big debt, I want to build something important".

When he started Alibaba, the cost of making a WEB-site was very high. He had almost no savings, so every decision was difficult. But Jack was clever. He told his partners: “we can not just spend money, we must also learn how to earn it”.

Step by step, Alibaba grew. The company helped people buy and sell online. Customers liked it because they could find a good price and sometimes even a discount.

Today, Jack Ma is one of the most famous businessmen in the world. His story shows that even if you start with little money, you can change your life with hard work.

Shopping day

Yesterday my family and I had a shopping day. my mom wanted a new washing machine because the old one was broken. She looked the price, but it was too high. She said, "I want a discount"!

My dad wanted to pay the electricity bill and keep the family budget under control. He also said he wants to check our savings, because our expenses are getting bigger.

My sister wanted new shoes, but she lost the recipt. So, she could not returen them. She wants to save money again.

I want a new vidoe game, but my pocket money is not anough. I don't want more debt, so I will not take a loan.

At the end of the day, we were tired, but we felt happy. We learned that shopping is not only about buying things, but also about planning and thingking about how we want to spend our money.
