Grammar: Past Simple with regular verbs

- We use "past simple " to talk about the past!

- Use ending -d if the verb ends with e letter.
Example: lived moved
We use -ed when the verb ends in most consonants or vowels
(except e or y with consonant before it).
Example: Watched played
If the verb ends in a consonant + y, change -y to -i and add -ied
Example: studied carried fried

Jackie Chan.

Jackie Chan is a famous actor. He was born in Hong Kong. He moved to a boarding school when he was 7 years old. He studied martial arts and acrobatics every day.

Later, Jackie watched many action movies and wanted to be like Bruce Lee. He worked very hard. He helped other actors and slowly became a star.

In his life, he visited many countries and explained Chinese culture to the world. He also rented small apartments when he was poor. He solved many problems with his humor.

Now, Jackie is loved all around the world. But many years ago, he just rested, trained, and never gave up. He is a true example of hard work!

Last weekend

Our last weekend was interesting. My little brother was ill, so we stayed at home. He was tired and sad because he did not go outside. We were hungry, but Mom made soup and we felt better. We talked about our childhood. I was happy most of the time.
My sister was tired after school, and I was often hungry because I didn’t like lunch at school.

Our teacher was very strict. She was not friendly, and we were a little afraid of her. But we had many friends, and we laughed a lot. Now, things are different. We are older. But we still remember the good days. Life was not always easy, but we were happy.
