We were happy.

Yesterday, we talked about our childhood. It was a long time ago, but we still remember many things. We were small, we were young, and we were full of energy. Life was very different back then. We didn’t have mobile phones or the internet. We played outside every day. Our favorite game was hide and seek. We were always excited to go outside after school. Even when we were tired, we played until it was dark.
Sometimes, we were hungry because we played too much and forgot to eat. Sometimes, one of us was ill and had to stay home. On those days, we were sad because we missed our friends. Our school was small, and our teacher was very strict. She wasn’t always kind, but she wanted us to learn. We were a little afraid of her, but now we understand she cared about us.
Even with the small problems — being tired, hungry, or sad — we were still happy. We were together. We had fun. We laughed a lot. Childhood wasn’t perfect, but in our hearts, we know that.

Last weekend

Last weekend was interesting. My little brother was ill, so we stayed at home. He was tired and sad because he couldn’t go outside. I was hungry, but Mom made soup and we felt better. We talked about our childhood. I was happy most of the time. My sister was sometimes tired after school, and I was often hungry because I didn’t like lunch at school.

Our teacher was very strict. She was not friendly, and we were a little afraid of her. But we had many friends, and we laughed a lot. Now, things are different. We are older. But we still remember the good days. Life was not always easy, but we were happy.
