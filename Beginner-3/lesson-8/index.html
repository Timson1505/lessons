The big companies in the world

There are many big companies in the world today. They make billions of dollars every year.
Apple is one of the most famous companies. It started in 1976. Today, Apple sells more than
200 000 000 of iPhones every year. The company has about 160 000 employees. Its brand is very strong and people respect it all over the world.

Amazon is another giant. Jeff Bezos started it in 1994 as a small online shop. Now, Amazon has more than 1 500 000 employees and serves millions of customers every day. In 2022, Amazon's income was more than 500 000 000 000 dollars.

Mercedes-Benz is one of the famous car brands in the world. It started in 1926 in Germany. Today, Mercedes sells cars in more than 150 countries. In 2022, the company sold about 2 500 000 000 cars.
Mrecedes-Benz has over than 170 000 employees around the world.
The company spends millions of euros every year on advertising and research. The price of this brand car can be from $40 000 to more than $200 000.
Mercedes-Benz is a strong brand and a big opportunity for business.

Nike is one of the biggest sport brands in the world. It started in 1964 in the USA.
In 2022, Nike made more than $46 000 000 000 in income. The company has more 79 000 employees. Nike sells shoes, clothes and sport equipment in 190 countries. Every year Nike spends millions of dollars to advertise. A pair of shoes can cost from $50 to $750.
Nike is a successful company, and many entrepreneurs respect its story.

A story of growth

MegaCom is a leading technology company that started as a startup in 2005. At the beginning, MegaCome had only 5 emplyees, but today it hires more than 12 000 people.

From the first year, MegaCom was faced with opportunities and risks. The company signed its first contract for $2 000 000. In 2010, MegaCom's income reached $500 000 000, and salary for employees was already $50 000 000.

MegaCom invests in advertising to grow its brand. Every year it spends $100 000 000 to advertise its service and product and reach new customers. The company also partners with local and international partners to explore new markets.

Today, MegaCom's income is $5 000 000 000 per-year. But its total expenses reach $3 000 000 000.

MegaCom proves that with hard work, smart decisions, a small startup can become a global brand.
