Exercise 1: Complete the sentences with suitable wh-questions.

  1. __________ did you buy at the market yesterday?
  2. __________ did she draw at the art class?
  3. __________ did you meet at the airport last night?
  4. __________ won the football match?
  5. __________ did he do after classes yesterday?
  6. __________ did they drink at the cafe this morning?
  7. __________ is the entrance to the museum?
  8. __________ sead did you choose on the plane?
  9. __________ was wrong with my homework?
  10. __________ did they do on holiday?

Exercise 3: Work with your partner, and discuss - what did you do last weekend?



My friend's weekend

Last weekend my friend had an interesting time. On Saturday, she bought a new dress at the mall. Then, she went to a park and met cousin there. They drew some pictures of some trees and flowers. It was sunny and warm, so they drank a bottle of cola and talked about school.

In the evening she went to a football match. Her favorite team won the game! She was very happy. After the game there was a big surprise - her friends gave her a birthday gift. She didn’t expect it.

On Sunday something went wrong. She forgot the keys to open the door. Fortunately, her brother came and helped her. They had lunch together.

Later, she went to the zoo. At the entrance, she saw many people. She waited in line and found a nice seat to watch an animal show. Unfortunately, it started to rain, so she came home early. But her weekend was funny.

My weekend

My weekend was really interesting. On Saturday morning I stayed at home. I wanted to draw a picture of mountains. My little sister helped me. Then, we drank hot chocolate and listened to music. In the afternoon we played a board game. I didn’t win, but it was so fun!

Later, I went to a store and bought some snacks. I met my uncle on the way home. He gave us a big surprise - tickets to the zoo!

On Sunday I went to the zoo with my sister. The line at the entrance was very long. We waited and fortunately, we got good seats. The lions and monkeys were very funny.

Unfortunately, our uncle lost his wallet. We looked everywhere, something went wrong. But a kind man found it and gave it back to us. We said “thank you” and went home tired but happy.
