Reading:

Our summer

Last summer, my friend Alex and I went to a small village near a lake. There were about two thousand people in the village, and everyone was very friendly. We stayed in an old hotel near the town center. There was a big park behind the hotel where children played every evening.
On the first day, we rented bikes and visited a museum. The guide, who was very funny, told us many interesting stories about the village. After that, we walked to a restaurant where we ate traditional food. I ordered an orange juice and Alex had a sandwich because he was very hungry.

The next morning, the weather was terrible, so we stayed inside the hotel for a few hours. Alex wanted to watch TV, but I wanted to read a book. Later, the rain stopped and we went outside again. We saw a woman who sold handmade souvenirs, and I bought a beautiful gift for my sister.

One evening, we asked a local man, “Where did the old castle disappear?” He laughed and said, “It burned down a hundred years ago!” We were very surprised because the castle was famous in the region.

Before we left, there was a music festival in the main square. There were hundreds of people, delicious food, and great music. We danced, laughed, and took many photos. It was an unforgettable trip, and now I want to go back there again one day.

Write short answers:

  1. Where did Alex and his friend go last summer?
  2. How many people were there in the village?
  3. Where was the hotel located?
  4. What did they do on the first day?
  5. Who told them interesting stories about the village?
  6. Why did Alex order a sandwich at the restaurant?
  7. What did the two friends do when the weather was terrible?
  8. What did the woman sell?
  9. What happened to the old castle?
  10. Why was the trip unforgettable for them?
