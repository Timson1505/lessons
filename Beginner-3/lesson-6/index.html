Benjamin Franklin

Benjamin Franklin was an important man in American History.

He was born in 1706 in Boston. He did not graduate from school, but he loved to read and learn new things. In 1723, when he was 17 years old, he moved to Philadelphia to start a new life.

Benjamin worked hard. In 1729 he published his own newspaper. Later, wrote “Poor Richard’s Almanack”, full of good advice. Many people respected him for his work and ideas. In 1730, he married a woman named Deborah. They lived together for many years and had children. Their life was not always easy. They had many problems. Some people say - they divorced.

Benjamin also participated in many events. In 1776, he helped to write a Declaration of Independence. In 1787, he worked with other leaders to write the U.S. Constitution. He got many awards in his life.

Chyngyz Aitmatov

Chyngyz Aitmatov was a famous writer from Kyrgyzstan. He was born in 1928 in a small village where many people worked on farms. He became a man who loved to read and write.

He graduated from a university in Moscow. Then he moved back to Kyrgyzstan, where he started to write books and stories. Many people respected his work. He also participated in meetings with other writers and leaders.

He got married and had children. They lived together in Bishkek. In his life he published many books, including “Jamila” an “The Day Lasts more than Hundred Years”. His books give you good advice and deep ideas.

He received more than one award for his great writing.
