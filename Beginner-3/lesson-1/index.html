New York

New York is a very big and famous city. Many people live there, and many tourists come to visit every year. Some people think New York is dangerous, but others feel it is safe. It is never boring — there is always something to do!

In the city, there are many streets. Some are narrow, and some are wide. There are places that are dirty and noisy, but there are also places that are pretty and clean. There are many tall skyscrapers. They look amazing, especially at night. There is a lot to see — New York is great for sightseeing. There are parks, museums, and famous places like the Statue of Liberty. It is never a poor place to visit.

Life in New York can be difficult and high in cost, but there is also a lot of fun. For many people, there is something special about New York. There is always something new to explore!

Budapest

Budapest is a beautiful city with many things to see. There are wide streets and narrow streets. There is a high skyscraper in the city center, and there are many beautiful sightseeing.

The city is safe, but some areas can be dirty and noisy. It is easy to travel around Budapest because there are many buses and trams. However, the city can be difficult to explore if you don’t know where to go.

Budapest is not boring at all. There are many parks and interesting places. Sometimes, there are poor people, but most parts of the city are clean and lively.
