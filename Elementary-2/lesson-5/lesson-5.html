Grammar: infinitive of purpose

to short, common, and used in everyday speech and writing.
in order to more formal, stronger, and often used when the purpose is very important or when you want to be extra clear.
✅ It's better to use to in the middle of sentences
❌ You cannot start a sentence with "to", use in order to instead

The role of education

Imagine two seeds. One falls on a dark, dry ground. The other falls into rich ground, gets sunlight, and is watered every day. Which one will grow into a strong tree? The answer is clear. People are like seeds. Education is the sunlight and water that helps us grow.

But why do people spend years studying? Why do governments pay for students’ education? And why is everyone from Brazil to Japan trying to learn English? Let’s explore.

Many people study to get a good job and earn more money. But the real reasons are deeper. First, education opens doors. A person with a degree in nursing, engineering, or IT can work almost anywhere. Second, education gives confidence. When you understand how the world works—from science to history—you feel less afraid of the future. Finally, education helps you solve real problems. It teaches you how to think, not just what to think. That is self-improvement at its best. Every effort you make to learn something new brings you closer to your goal.

You might ask: Why does the government care if I study? Why do they build schools, pay teachers, and give free books? The answer is simple: A smart country is a rich and safe country. When people are educated, they get better jobs, pay more taxes, and need less help from the state. They also make better decisions. An educated person is less likely to believe fake news or follow dangerous ideas. In short, education creates responsible citizens. That is a huge profit for any government.

Education is not only for work. It is for life.

It teaches you to manage money, bring up children, understand your health, and enjoy art. It helps you ask better questions. For example, instead of saying, “Why is this happening to me?” you ask, “What can I learn from this?” That small change is the heart of self-improvement.

Also, education protects your freedom. History shows that uneducated people are easier to control. When you can read, think critically, and find information, no one can easily take away your privacy or lie to you. Education gives you access to truth and good decisions.

Walk into any cafe in Berlin, Buenos Aires, or Bangkok. You will hear people studying English on their phones. Why?
English is the key to the world. Most scientific papers are written in English. The biggest websites, movies, and business meetings use English. If you learn English, you can:
Study at a top university in London or New York.
Work for an international company without leaving your city.
Talk to people from 100 different countries.

In short, English is not just a language. It is a tool for connection and opportunity. When you know English, you contribute your ideas to the world, not just to your local community.

The main lesson is simple: Education does not end when you finish school. It continues every time you read a book, watch a documentary, or learn a new skill. Governments help because they need educated people. But the real profit is yours: a richer, freer, and more interesting life.

So, be like that seed. Find your sunlight. Keep learning. Keep improving. Your future self will thank you.

The advantage and disadvantage of technology

Today, technology plays a main role in our daily lives. People use different devices and applications to achieve their personal and professional goals. For example, students use the internet to access information and learn new skills, while workers use technology in order to complete their tasks faster and with less effort.

Many people also use technology to earn money. They work online, create content, or run businesses in order to make a profit. At the same time, technology helps people communicate. Friends and families use messaging apps to have conversations and stay connected, even if they live far away. Parents often use technology to bring up their children by showing them educational videos or helping them study.

Technology is also important for society. The government uses it to improve public services and to contribute to the country’s development. For example, online systems are created in order to provide better education, healthcare, and communication.

However, technology has disadvantages too. Many people spend too much time online to relax, but they often waste hours without any real purpose. Instead of using technology to improve their lives, they use it to scroll through social media or play games all day. This can reduce productivity and affect their health.

Another problem is privacy. People share personal information online to connect with others, but sometimes this information is not safe. Companies may use personal data in order to earn money, which can create risks for users.

In conclusion, technology is like a seed that can grow into something useful if people use it wisely. It helps individuals and society to develop, but only if it is used with clear purpose. Otherwise, people may lose time and opportunities instead of gaining benefits.
