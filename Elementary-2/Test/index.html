Reading:

A Busy Day in Modern Life

Nowadays, everybody wants to live a happier and healthier life. Many people would like to have more free time, but life is becoming busier and busier. Sometimes we need somebody to help us or something to make our daily routines easier.

Last weekend, Emma decided to change her lifestyle in order to feel better and less stressed. She woke up earlier than usual to go jogging in the park. The weather was colder than the day before, but she still felt energetic. She ran quickly and listened to music quietly while the city was waking up.

After jogging, Emma went somewhere peaceful to drink tea and read a book. She believes that reading is more relaxing than spending hours on social media. She also started eating healthier food because she wants to become stronger and more active.

Later that day, Emma met somebody from her old school. Her friend looked happier and more confident than before. They talked about their dreams and future plans. Emma said, “I would like to travel to the most beautiful countries in the world one day.”

In the evening, Emma watched a documentary to learn new ideas about healthy living. She learned that the simplest habits are sometimes the most effective. Before going to bed, she wrote something important in her notebook: “Small changes can make life better.”

1. Emma decided to change her lifestyle because she wanted to feel __________.

a) richer
b) better and less stressed
c) more famous

2. Emma woke up earlier than usual to __________.

a) study
b) go shopping
c) go jogging

3. The weather was __________ than the day before.

a) hotter
b) colder
c) sunnier

4. Emma listened to music __________ while she was running.

a) loudly
b) badly
c) quietly

5. After jogging, Emma went somewhere __________.

a) noisy
b) peaceful
c) crowded

6. Emma thinks reading is more relaxing than __________.

a) watching TV
b) playing sports
c) spending hours on social media

7. Later that day, Emma met somebody from her __________.

a) office
b) family
c) old school

8. Emma’s friend looked __________ than before.

a) sadder
b) happier and more confident
c) more tired

9. Emma said she would like to travel to the __________ countries in the world.

a) cheapest
b) coldest
c) most beautiful

10. Before going to bed, Emma wrote something important in her __________.

a) notebook
b) phone
