Hollywood

When you hear "Hollywood," you probably think of red carpets, famous actors, and huge movie studios. But did you know that Hollywood started with a very different idea?

In 1886, a man named Harvey Wilcox and his wife, Daeida, bought a large piece of land in California. Harvey’s first idea was not to make movies. He wanted to create a quiet, religious community where people could live a simple, moral life. He named the area "Hollywood" after the beautiful holly bushes he had seen on a trip.

But the plan failed. The community stayed small, and nobody wanted to move there. Then, a big change happened. In the early 1900s, filmmakers moved to California mainly to escape strict rules and expensive licenses controlled by Thomas Edison. They wanted to work freely and creatively. California was a perfect place because the weather was usually sunny, and people could film movies easily almost every day. This decision slowly but surely turned a quiet area into a busy film industry.

At first, Hollywood was just a small and peaceful village. But it quickly grew into a crowded and exciting place where actors, directors, and writers worked tirelessly. Studios were built fast, and movies became more popular year by year.

One of the most surprising and inspiring stories from Hollywood is about Marilyn Monroe. She was not born into a rich or stable family. In fact, her early life was extremely difficult, and she lived poorly for many years. At the beginning, she worked quietly as a model and was often ignored by major studios.

However, she did not give up. She worked incredibly hard and improved her acting gradually. People started to notice her natural beauty and charming personality. Eventually, she became one of the most famous and admired actresses in the world. She spoke softly, smiled brightly, and acted beautifully, which made her unforgettable.

So, next time you see the Hollywood sign, remember: this place of fame began with a quiet dream of a religious town, and its biggest stars often started with nothing. Hollywood may look perfect and shiny, but its history is actually complex and sometimes difficult. It began with brave people who wanted to create something new and worked passionately to achieve their dreams.

Homework

The habit which brought to success

Arnold Schwarzenegger was born in a small village in Austria. As a child, he was curious about the world and dreamed of a bigger life. He was also a bit shy, especially at school, but inside he had a strong desire to succeed. Sometimes he was impatient because he wanted success quickly, but this feeling pushed him to work harder.
Arnold’s family was strict, and his father could be very serious. Life was not always easy, but Arnold learned to be brave and strong. He was also honest with himself — he knew he wanted to become someone important. Unlike some people around him, he refused to be selfish. Instead, he believed in helping others and later became very generous with his time and money.
As a teenager, Arnold discovered bodybuilding. He trained every day and was extremely reliable — he never missed a workout. People sometimes thought he was too confident or even a little rude, but in reality, he was just very focused. With his creative thinking, he found new ways to improve his body and performance.
When Arnold moved to the United States, he didn’t speak English well, and life was difficult. However, he stayed kind and polite to others, even when people laughed at his accent. Step by step, he achieved success — first as a bodybuilding champion, then as a famous actor, and later as a political leader.
Arnold Schwarzenegger’s story shows that success is not only about talent. It is about character: being brave, reliable, and never giving up. Even if you start as a shy child with big dreams, you can achieve great things with hard work and the right attitude.
