The warning of Edward Snowden

Do you check your phone when you wake up? Look around, how many people are looking at the screen? Technology is a big part of our lives, especially Social Media.

Edward Snowden worked for the US government. In 2013, he shared a big secret. He showed that the government and big companies are watching people online, all the time. Snowden warns us to be careful, he says:
- Social Media and Apps collect a lot of information about you!
- When you post, upload photos, or write a comment, you are sharing your life.
- Your password and data are not always private!

When you post or upload, you share data that can be collected and watched. Protect your information with a strong password, this is the key part of cybersecurity. Snowden also warns about problems like Screen Addiction - when you cannot stop looking at your device.

The message from Snowden: Technology is a tool, you must be a smart user. Think carefully before you share anything online. Use strong cybersecurity, manage your time to avoid screed addiction, and remember that what you post can last forever. The goal is to use your device to connect and learn, without letting it control you or take your private information.
