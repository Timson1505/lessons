My favorite day

My favorite day of the week is Saturday, because it’s the weekend! I usually wake up at 9.00 a.m., but I get up late at 10.30. Then I have breakfast and read the book. My parents don’t work this day. My mother cooks a big lunch. After lunch we play cards or watch TV. There is a park near our flat. My parents sometimes go for a walk, but I don’t go with them, I don’t like walking or doing exercise. I stay at home and surf the internet or chat with friends.

In the evening my sister’s family comes home. They usually arrive at 6:15 o’clock in the evening. My sister’s name is Amanda. Nicole is Amanda’s daughter and Fred is Amanda’s husband. Amanda and I help my mum to cook dinner. My father and Fred like playing chess. We have dinner together. Then I play with my niece. At about 9:45 my sister’s family leaves the house. They go their home. They don’t take a bus, they drive a car. We don’t work on Sunday, that’s why we never sleep until 01.00 a.m.
