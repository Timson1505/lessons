Bruno Mars

Bruno Mars is a famous singer in the world. Many people love him for his amazing voice and energy on stage. When he plays the guitar or piano, people can’t stop watching him.

His songs are full of feeling and fans love to sing them at every concert. He uses a microphone to make his voice stronger. He writes his own lyrics and records them in the studio. When people download his songs, they listen again and again.

Bruno is also a very kind person. He often thanks his fans. He can play almost any instrument.

Once, he participated in a music contest when he was young - and everyone knew he would become famous.

Me and my brother Tim

He is crazy about singing karaoke. He downloads his favorite song lyrics and sings along.

My voice is not perfect, but sometimes I like practicing too. I also play an instrument. Playing and singing together with my brother is too much fun!Also, we joined a singing contest. I think we need to record our voice
