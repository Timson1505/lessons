My family’s day in London

My name is Luke. My hobby is photography. I like it very much. When I have time, I take my camera and take many pictures. I take photos when I am at my grandparents'. They live in the village and have a big garden.

I get up in the morning and go to the garden to take pictures. Sometimes, I take pictures of birds, animals and send them to magazines. I do not like taking pictures of people. I also like playing computer games and board games. We have a computer at home, my father brings a laptop and we play games together. My favorite games are strategy games. I do not like war games.

The forest children

Alan Sendler is 43 years old and he has four children: two daughters and two sons. John is a scientist and he lives with three of his children in the South American forest. Their house is a group of tents near the River Orinoco in Venezuela. John’s wife and one daughter live in London.

They get up at 5 o’clock in the morning. Children help their father to cook breakfast. John’s children don’t go to school, because John is their teacher. He teaches them everyday. After John’s classes, they have lunch and go to the river for fishing. The children don’t know how to use the playstation, but they all drive a car. His 9 years old son Simon too. At night they drive their car, because there is a lot of news on the radio.

They don’t have a TV or CD player. In the summer, the children’s friends come from London to visit. When they go home, they tell their parents interesting stories of their holidays in the Venezuela forest.
