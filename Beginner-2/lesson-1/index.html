Whose?

Grammar: Possessive 's

- Use possessive 's to show whose… (belongings)
This is Temirlan's pen This is my friend's car
My father's name is Tilek My sister's camera is black
Rodolfo is Rosa's husband Rosa is Rodolfo's wife
Pedro is Yo's brother Yo is Pedro's sister
Filipe is Yo and Pedro's father Ana is Pedro and Yo's sister
- Do not forget that "'s" can be verb to be.
Temirlan's a teacher. Temirlan is a teacher.
