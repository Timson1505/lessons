My friend's habit

My friend has many interesting habits, both good and bad. One good habit he always tries to improve himself. He usually wears comfortable clothes, so he can work or study for a long time without feeling tired. He often practices English in the evening, it helps him to build confidence and develop communication skills.

However, he sometimes has some bad habits too. He rarely decides things quickly and often wants to discuss every small detail before making a choice. Sometimes he doesn’t trust his ideas and asks for advice.

But he is a really friendly and kind person. He always supports his friends and family. He rarely hikes in mountains, but likes going for a picnic.

My friend is not perfect, his habits help him to grow. His good habits are strong enough to improve his life, and his bad habits remind him that there is always room to change.

My new friend

I have a new friend. Her name is Naomi, and she is from Poland. She moved to Bishkek two months ago. Now, we always see each other almost every weekend, and it has become a nice habit for both of us.

Naomi is very friendly and easy to talk to. She often speaks English with me, and she sometimes uses Kyrgyz language because she wants to improve her language skills. She regularly practices new words in daily conversations. Over time, she is developing confidence and building strong communication skills.

Naomi usually wears comfortable clothes, especially when we meet after a long day. She often cooks delicious vegetarian meals and sometimes creates new recipes. She always supports me when I want to try something new, and we often discuss our plans before we decide what to do.

However, there are some things Naomi can’t do yet. She never drives a car, so she usually takes the bus or walks. She rarely goes swimming because she is afraid of water. One day, I want to support her and help her develop confidence in swimming.
