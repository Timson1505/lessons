My day in Budapest

My routine is the same everyday. I wake up early in the morning at 6 o’clock. Then I go to do morning exercise. I have my breakfast and listen to music. I go to university. I have lunch at the university. For lunch I have only salad, sandwich and coffee or tea.

After university, I do my homework and go to play football or meet with friends. In the evening I cook dinner for my family. There are 5 people: my grandmother, my parents, me and my sister. After dinner, we watch TV then I go to sleep.

Work and family

My name is Timson. I live with my brother, Adam. He is married. We live with his wife and his daughter. So, we are a big family. I get up at 6 o’clock in the morning. I go to university. I study programming. After university I meet with my friend Liza. We have lunch. Then I go home. I do my homework and play with Bella. Bella is Adam’s daughter and Mary is Adam’s wife.

I watch and listen to music. At 11 o’clock I go to sleep. Flats are not very expensive in Budapest, so we live in the center of the city. Our flat is small and there are many nice things in it: TV, internet, many books, pictures. Our parents live in Kyrgyzstan. They have a big beautiful house. My parents don’t work. I have a lot of friends here in Hungary. My friends speak Hungarian and English. I speak English but I don’t speak Hungarian. I play football with my brother and friends at the weekend.
