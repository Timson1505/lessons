My favorite day

My favorite time of the day is the morning because I get up early in the morning at 5:30 and feel full of energy. I work in a big American company in Chicago. I am an IT-manager. My favorite day of the week is Friday because it’s the end of the week and I can go home for the weekend. I am from a small town Sun-lake near the Chicago and my parents live there. My favorite month is July because I have my vacation and I can spend a lot of time in Sun-lake. There I usually relax, swim in the lake and play volleyball on the beach. My favorite season is spring. The summer is very hot in Chicago, but in the spring the temperature is very nice. I love our garden in Sun-lake. There are many green trees and beautiful flowers. I feel myself very comfortable. My favorite holiday is my birthday, because this day I am in the center of people’s attention. There are many gifts from my parents and friends.

  1. What is his favorite time of the day? Why?
  2. What is his favorite day of the week? Why?
  3. What is his favorite month? Why?
  4. What is his favorite season? Why?
  5. What is his favorite holiday? Why?
