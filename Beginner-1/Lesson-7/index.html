Exercise 1: Write is or are

  1. There _________ a lot of parks in Budapest.
  2. There _________ big hotels near my house.
  3. There _________ restraunt here.
  4. There _________ people in the street.
  5. There _________ cheap ticket for the museum.
  6. There _________ tree in the garden.
  7. There _________ students in the classroom.
  8. There _________ nice cafes in Bishkek.

Use SOME for positive, ANY for negative and question forms!

Some (+) Any (-) Any (?)
There are some people in the park There are not any people in the park Are there any people in the park?
There are some papers on the table There are not any papers on the table Are there any papers on the table?
