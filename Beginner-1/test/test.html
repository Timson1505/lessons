Reading:

Anna is my best friend. This is Anna’s dog, Max. He is very funny and friendly. Anna lives in a small house in the city, and she goes to school every day at 8 o’clock. I study with her because we are in the same class.

We usually meet on Fridays at the café on Green Street. Sometimes we do homework together, but we often just talk and laugh. Anna likes music and dancing, but she doesn’t like basketball. I love sports, so we are a little different.

“Where does Anna live?” my cousin sometimes asks. She lives near the park, and her house is next to a big supermarket.

Right now, Anna is sitting in her room and doing her homework. I am writing a message to her. We are planning a party for Saturday night.

At the party, there are many snacks and drinks. How many pizzas do we need? How much juice should we buy? Anna says we need five pizzas because many friends are coming.

My bag is black, and hers is blue. That guitar near the window is mine. Anna can play the guitar very well, but I can’t play it at all.

It is quarter past seven now, and we are getting ready for the party!

1. Who is Max?

a) Anna's dog
b) Anna's brother
c) Anna's teacher

2. Where does Anna live?

a) Near the school
b) Near the park
c) Near the hospital

3. What time does Anna go to school?

a) At 7 o'clock
b) At 8 o'clock
c) At 9 o'clock

4. What does Anna like?

a) Basketball
b) Music and dancing
c) Tennis

5. Why are Anna and her friend a little different?

a) Because they go to different schools
b) Because they like different things
c) Because they live in different cities

6. What is Anna doing right now?.

a) Watching TV
b) Doing her homework
c) Playing basketball

7. How many pizzas do they need for the party?

a) three
b) four
c) five

8. Which bag is blue?

a) Anna’s bag
b) The writer’s bag
c) Max’s bag

9. Who can play the guitar very well?

a) The writer
b) Anna
c) Anna’s cousin

10. What time is it at the end of the text?

a) Quarter past seven
b) Half past seven
c) Quarter to seven
