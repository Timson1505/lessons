My mother’s birthday

My name is Andras. Today is my mother’s birthday. We celebrate it at home. We invite our neighbors and friends. Our neighbors Virag and Mark are from Budapest, they are teachers. Jim and Rose are sellers, they are from the USA. They are all nice people.

They give my mother birthday cards, presents, and flowers. They say wishes and sing a song. We have a beautiful and tasty cake with two candles. My mother makes a wish and blows them. We take photos, dance and eat. We like my mother’s birthday.
