Grammar: Modal verbs

- Use should to give advice or recommendation.
- Use have to for necessity, (when you don't want, but you have to).

I make my own history

Conor McGregor was born in Dublin, Ireland, in 1988. His childhood was not easy. His family did not have much money, so everyone had to work hard and show strong commitment. As a boy, Conor sometimes helped at home by doing chores like sweeping the floor, mopping the floor, doing the laundry, and taking out the trash. His parents taught him that children should respect their family and help with responsibilities at home.

Before becoming famous, Conor worked as a plumber. Every morning, he had to wake up very early and travel long distances to work. The job was exhausting. After work, he still went to the gym because he believed he must follow his dream of becoming a fighter. Many people warned him that UFC was too dangerous and that he should choose a safer career. Some even forbade him from wasting time on fighting, but Conor refused to give up.

Life was difficult at that time. He and his girlfriend often struggled financially. Sometimes they barely had enough money to pay bills. Conor understood that success must come from discipline and hard work. While other people relaxed after work, he trained for hours. He knew he had to stay focused.

Eventually, Conor McGregor became one of the biggest stars in UFC history. He won championship belts in two weight divisions and became famous around the world. His story shows that people should never stop believing in themselves. If someone wants to achieve great success, they must work hard and have to overcome many difficulties along the way.

Homework

In my family, everyone has to help with household chores because we believe that keeping the house clean is a shared responsibility. My parents say that children should learn responsibility from an early age. Every member of the family has different tasks and responsibilities at home.

My mother usually cooks meals and does the laundry, but we all help her when she is busy. My father often repairs things around the house and sometimes takes out the trash. I usually sweep the floor, mop the floor, and tidy up my room. My sister likes to dust the furniture and water the plants. We also have to feed our cat every morning and evening.

At home, there are some rules that we must follow. We mustn’t leave dirty dishes in the kitchen, and we must clean our rooms every weekend. My parents often warn us that a messy house can create stress and problems. They also forbid us from using phones during dinner because family time is important.

Sometimes helping at home is difficult, especially after school or work, but we understand that everyone should contribute. Household chores teach us discipline, teamwork, and commitment. I think children should help their parents because these responsibilities prepare them for adult life.
