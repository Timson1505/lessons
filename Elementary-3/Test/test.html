Reading:

An exchange program in abroad

Maria has been part of a student exchange program in Germany for six months, and her life has changed completely. Before she arrived, she had never traveled abroad alone, so she was nervous about living with a host family in a foreign country. On the first day, she realized that she must speak German as much as possible if she wanted to improve quickly. Her host parents told her that she should not be afraid of making mistakes because practice was the best teacher.

At school, Maria has made many new friends, and she has already visited several famous places. She even went to a small town near the Alps during a school trip. The teachers explained that students have to follow strict rules during excursions. If someone breaks the rules, they will not be allowed to join future activities.

Sometimes Maria thinks about how different her life would be if she had not joined the program. If she had stayed in her hometown, she would never have met her best friend Anna. Now she feels more confident, and if she were younger, she would probably be too shy to travel alone. She also regrets one thing: if she had studied more German before coming to Germany, she would have understood the lessons more easily at the beginning.

Last week, Maria lost her passport while visiting a museum in Berlin. She was terrified because she thought she would have to return home immediately. Fortunately, a tourist found the passport and gave it to the police. If the man had not helped her, she might still be looking for it now.

Maria believes the exchange program has been the most important experience of her life. She has learned that students should try new experiences even when they are difficult, because they can completely change the future.
